# Job Portals Directory 🔗

The best job, internship, and freelance portals for all backgrounds—each chosen for reliability and audience fit.

## 🌍 Global/General Portals

- [LinkedIn Jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/) — *Largest professional network; easy recruiter access.*
- [Indeed](https://www.indeed.com/) — *The biggest job aggregator worldwide, covers all industries.*
- [Foundit (Monster India)](https://www.foundit.in/) — *Legacy platform for fresher and experienced jobs in India/APAC.*
- [Wellfound (AngelList Talent)](https://wellfound.com/) — *World’s best for startups, remote work, and tech roles.*
- [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Job/index.htm) — *Search jobs and see anonymous company reviews and salary reports.*
- [CutShort](https://cutshort.io/) — *Fast-growing Indian tech/startup job board—AI-powered matchmaking.*
- [Shine.com](https://www.shine.com/) — *Oldest Indian aggregator; freshers to execs.*
- [ZipRecruiter](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/) — *Easy US/UK/CA/Europe jobs; high-quality filters.*
- [SimplyHired](https://www.simplyhired.com/) — *US/global, intuitive aggregator.*
- [FlexJobs](https://www.flexjobs.com/) — *Top-rated for remote/flexible positions, entry to senior.*
- [USAJobs.gov](https://www.usajobs.gov/) — *Official US Government jobs—many paid internships!*
- [Seek](https://www.seek.com.au/) — *Major Australia/New Zealand portal.*
- [JobStreet](https://www.jobstreet.com/) — *Asia-Pacific focus, trusted by local recruiters.*
- [Europe Language Jobs](https://www.europelanguagejobs.com/) — *Multi-language jobs in Europe, all levels.*
- [RemoteOK](https://remoteok.io/) — *Curated remote jobs, frequently updated, tech-centered.*
- [We Work Remotely](https://weworkremotely.com/) — *Biggest pure-remote platform, all industries.*
- [Internshala](https://internshala.com/) — *India’s #1 internship and fresher platform.*
- [Freshersworld](https://www.freshersworld.com/) — *Campus jobs, IT, and walk-in interview alerts (India).*
- [HyreInsta](https://hyreinsta.com/) — *India’s rising star for fresher and bulk hiring programs.*
- [JobHai](https://www.jobhai.com/) — *Entry-level, blue-collar, and non-tech jobs for India.*
- [Angel.co](https://angel.co/) — *Startup jobs, global/remote, rebranded as Wellfound.*
- [GitHub Jobs (archive)](https://jobs.github.com/) — *Reference for developer job trends, now closed but useful for role inspiration.*
