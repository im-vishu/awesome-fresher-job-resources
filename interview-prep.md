## 💡 Tips & Success Strategies

1. Build a balance of timed problem-solving + “explaining your code out loud.”
2. Write code on whiteboard/paper/Notepad to mimic real-world interview setting.
3. Create a “last week” cheat sheet—review must-know concepts and favorite Qs.
4. Practice explaining your projects: what, why, how, and your unique contribution.
5. For behavioral Qs: use the STAR structure and have 3 real stories ready (project, teamwork, conflict).

## 📅 Sample Weekly Plan

- Mon-Tue: 2–3 LeetCode patterns (e.g., Arrays, Strings)
- Wed: Mock system/HR interview via Pramp or peer
- Thu: 2 company-specific interview reports (Glassdoor/GeeksforGeeks)
- Fri: 5 behavioral questions aloud/self-video record
- Sat-Sun: 1 mock contest + review failed solutions
